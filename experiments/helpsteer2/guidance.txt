These ratings follow the HelpSteer2 annotation conventions. Rate each attribute independently, on the whole response.
- First check the response carefully for factual errors, wrong calculations, hallucinated details, accepting a false premise in the prompt, and violations of explicit instructions in the prompt (requested length, number of sentences/words, format, "use the reference text", what to include or exclude). These matter most.
- correctness: 4 = everything correct and all instructions followed; 3 = small omissions or minor inaccuracies; 2 = some real errors or a missed requirement; 0-1 = the core answer is wrong, hallucinated, accepts a false premise, or ignores the main instruction.
- helpfulness tracks correctness closely: a fluent response with a wrong core answer or that ignores the main instruction is 0-1. A correct, complete response that does what was asked is 4. Most responses are 3-4.
- coherence: most responses are 4 (clear and consistent). Use 3 for some awkwardness, repetition or minor inconsistency; 2 or lower only for genuinely confusing, contradictory or broken text.
- complexity: about the language/knowledge needed to write the response, not its correctness. Most responses are 1 (everyday) or 2 (some education in the topic, e.g. typical technical, professional or academic writing). 3 needs real expertise (e.g. specialized code, law, medicine); 4 is rare. Very short or trivial replies are 0.
- verbosity: length and detail relative to what the prompt asks for. 2 is typical (appropriate length). 3 = longer or more detailed than needed; 4 = very long/padded. 1 = brief; 0 = one line or a few words.
